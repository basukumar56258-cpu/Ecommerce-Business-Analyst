# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project
Sales & Customer Performance Analytics

## Business Problem
Management needs a single view of sales, profitability, customer and regional performance to identify growth opportunities and underperforming segments.

## Objectives
- Monitor revenue and profit.
- Identify high-performing regions and categories.
- Track order and customer performance.
- Highlight low-margin areas.
- Support data-driven decisions.

## Stakeholders
Business Manager, Sales Manager, Finance Team, Operations Team, Business Analyst.

## Functional Requirements
1. Filter by date, region and category.
2. Display revenue, profit, margin, orders and AOV.
3. Show monthly trends.
4. Show regional and category performance.
5. Show customer performance.
6. Export filtered data.

## Acceptance Criteria
- KPI values update when filters change.
- Charts reflect selected filters.
- Valid CSV data loads without manual code changes.
- Export contains the currently filtered records.
